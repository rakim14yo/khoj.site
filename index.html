How to read the numbers

  • Every rate shows how long ago it was last confirmed. Anything past 6 months is marked as possibly outdated — rent drifts, and we'd rather say so than pretend it hasn't.
  • The "typical rent" for an area is a median built from recent, un-flagged reports, not one exact figure — real markets are ranges, not single numbers.
  • Entries that sit far outside an area's usual range, or get flagged by several visitors, stay visible but are left out of that area's median so a handful of odd entries can't skew it.
  • Nothing here is verified against a lease or a landlord. Treat it as a strong starting point for negotiation, not an appraisal.
খোঁজ Khoj is an independent, unverified, anonymous board for Chattogram renters. Every rate is self-reported — we don't check it against a lease, an owner, or a listing site. It exists to give the next renter a realistic sense of what's normal before they negotiate, not to make a claim about any specific building or owner. Reports that are flagged repeatedly are kept visible but marked, never silently deleted.
