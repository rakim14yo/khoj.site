How to read the numbers

  • Every rate shows how long ago it was last confirmed. Anything past 6 months is marked as possibly outdated — rent drifts, and we'd rather say so than pretend it hasn't.
  • Area prices are shown as a range built from recent, un-flagged reports, not one exact figure — real markets are ranges, not single numbers.
  • Rates that sit far outside an area's usual range, or get flagged by several visitors, are kept visible but excluded from that area's median so a few odd entries can't skew it.
  • This board isn't cross-checked against every listing site automatically — treat it as a strong starting point for negotiation, not a verified appraisal.
Khoj is an independent, unverified board — every rate is self-reported and not checked against a lease or a landlord. It exists to give renters a rough sense of what's normal before they negotiate, not to make a claim about any specific building or owner.
